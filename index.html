04 — Locked Rooms

05 — Assignment Rules

01Excluded from rooming: Restaurant & Bar Supervisor, Sales & Service Manager, Onboard Hotel Manager — these positions are left out of the pool entirely.
02Everyone gets a bed, no exceptions: the top priority, above all else. If every other option runs out, the algorithm will pair Basic staff together, pair Basic with Seasonal, or even split one person's stint across more than one room mid-turn — always flagged clearly when it happens.
03No mixed-gender rooms: a cabin never holds overlapping occupants of different declared genders — this is the one rule that's never relaxed, even as a last resort.
04Otherwise, minimize disruption: Hotel Staff Basic get first claim on a solo room when there's spare capacity, Hotel Staff Seasonal get the same shot at whatever's left rather than being packed together while rooms sit empty, and everyone keeps one room for their whole stint whenever possible — these are strong preferences, just not stronger than rule 02.

09 — Room Assignments
